## Project Overview

The Stellantis Industrial Physical AI Robot Demonstrator is an Arizona State University and Wandelbots collaboration developed for **Stellantis Factory Booster Day 2026** in Detroit. The demonstrator combines two UR5e industrial robots, machine vision, industrial networking, and simulation-to-real integration to showcase physical AI for manufacturing.

## My Contributions

I am responsible for:

- Setting up and integrating the dual-UR5e hardware demonstrator.
- Supporting machine-vision setup and validation.
- Configuring and testing the industrial network.
- Developing and integrating Isaac Sim and NVIDIA Omniverse components.
- Performing end-to-end system testing across hardware, software, and communications.
