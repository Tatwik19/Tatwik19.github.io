## Course Description

This three-credit course covers the principles and applications of industrial robotic systems, including robot classification, control, programming, simulation, system integration, and deployment in modern manufacturing environments.

## Student Learning Outcomes

- Apply kinematic and dynamic principles to industrial robotic systems.
- Analyze, simulate, and program industrial robots.
- Develop robotic solutions for manufacturing applications.
- Integrate AI methods into industrial robotic systems.
- Evaluate robot workspaces, tooling, safety, and deployment requirements.
